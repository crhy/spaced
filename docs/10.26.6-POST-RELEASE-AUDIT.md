# 10.26.6 post-release issue audit — 23 October 2026

This is a new status assessment after publication, not an amendment to the
[release-time review](10.26.6-RELEASE-REVIEW.md) or published changelog.
The [release](https://github.com/crhy/spaced/releases/tag/v10.26.6),
[illustrated announcement](https://github.com/crhy/spaced/discussions/369)
and [changes since 10.26.1](https://github.com/crhy/spaced/discussions/369#discussioncomment-19156192)
are public. See [subsystem ownership](SUBSYSTEMS.md) and the
[Compiz investigation and recommendation](COMPIZ.md).

## Resolved report

[#341](https://github.com/crhy/spaced/issues/341), the update warning / second
click report, is resolved by the recovery work tracked in
[#337](https://github.com/crhy/spaced/issues/337), merged as `1499592` and
released in 10.26.5, retained in 10.26.6. The helper configures pending
packages, checks dependency health and retries the same APT transaction
once. Persistent errors still fail. The release evidence includes a
10.26.4-to-10.26.5 workstation upgrade in one transaction, clean `dpkg --audit`
and recovery tests. The reporter also confirmed on 21 October that #341 was
already fixed and left open as a notice. Close it as completed and remove
the misleading `wontfix` label. The missing original first-error log does not
justify blaming duplicate APT sources or claiming all APT failures fixed.

Recently resolved release issues already have closure/release comments;
they do not need duplicate comments. #338 was closed as a duplicate of
#158, which remains unimplemented. Closed #183 receives the new Compiz
strategy reference; its closed state does not mean a fork was created.

## Separate repositories

The audit also reviewed all six open Welcome/Bazaar issues and all six open
issues in the separate `crhy/spacedupdate` repository. None has sufficient
delivery evidence for closure:

- Welcome [#11](https://github.com/crhy/spacedwelcome/issues/11): the current
  suggested-app choice is not the requested highlighted/pulsing control.
- Bazaar [#27](https://github.com/crhy/spacedbazaar/issues/27),
  [#17](https://github.com/crhy/spacedbazaar/issues/17),
  [#15](https://github.com/crhy/spacedbazaar/issues/15),
  [#10](https://github.com/crhy/spacedbazaar/issues/10) and
  [#9](https://github.com/crhy/spacedbazaar/issues/9): APT variants,
  application/dark screenshots, curated voting and P2P remain separate work.
- Spaced Update [#17](https://github.com/crhy/spacedupdate/issues/17): current
  `spaced-themes` ships self-contained GRUB assets and the update helper runs
  `update-grub`, but that does not establish migration of an old installed
  machine's `GRUB_THEME` setting. Reproduce the original upgrade before
  closure; do not equate the corrected ISO with a proven migration.
- Spaced Update [#16](https://github.com/crhy/spacedupdate/issues/16):
  metadata-refresh fixes alone do not prove the named application's release
  reached its installed Flatpak origin and scope. Verify publication and
  update end to end.
- Spaced Update [#6](https://github.com/crhy/spacedupdate/issues/6),
  [#5](https://github.com/crhy/spacedupdate/issues/5),
  [#4](https://github.com/crhy/spacedupdate/issues/4) and
  [#2](https://github.com/crhy/spacedupdate/issues/2): theme sharing, file
  sharing and P2P/privacy proposals remain unimplemented.

The distribution installs Update from this main repository's
`overlays/usr/lib/spaced-linux/`. Implementing a fix only in the separate
Update repository does not ship it. Independently maintained applications
such as Voxa need their own release evidence; this audit does not claim to
close or exhaustively classify every application repository's backlog.
